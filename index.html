    About AlisaInks✦

    Who I am

    As cliché as it sounds, I've always loved writing. Books were my escape from a young age, and writing became something even more personal. I started with poetry, and whenever I've found myself in a dark place, writing has always been where I return. It was, in some way, always in my blood.

    Finding Wattpad in 2021 was one of the best things that happened to me. Honestly, the whole thing was a little ridiculous. I stumbled across the app before I even had a phone of my own. I read my first Wattpad book on my mother's phone, and from there, I was hooked. I would stay up all night reading, borrowing her phone whenever I could. Eventually, exams and real life got in the way, but those nights are still a sweet memory.

    I write mainly MM sensory fiction and literary romance, with a particular love for stories that linger long after you've finished them.

    My all-time favourite book, Perfume by Patrick Süskind, has influenced me more than almost anything else I've read. I have a weakness for writing that is intensely descriptive, sensory, strange, and impossible to shake. I can forgive a book for having a weak plot. I have a much harder time forgiving it for making me feel nothing.

    For me, that's what good fiction does. It makes you feel something. It leaves an impression.

    I want my stories to stay with you.
    Even if you can't quite explain why.

    How I rate a book

    Plot
    Characters
    Feels
    Writing

    I've read books where the plot had holes and the characters were nothing special, but the feelings stayed. I always believe you remember a book more by how it made you feel.

    My writing, then and now

    I'm quite proud of where I've come. But if you saw how I wrote in 2023, you'd cringe so hard you'd shrivel.

    I think fiction can shape reality just as much as it can reflect it. What we read, watch, listen to, and surround ourselves with becomes part of the way we see the world. Stories can influence what we notice, what we question, what we empathize with, and sometimes even who we become.

    Where my ideas come from

    • Book/movie lines
    • Music
    • Random 2am thoughts
    • Doomscrolling

    What I try to give

    • An immersive world for my readers
    • Enough small details to notice
    • Areas to form your own interpretation
    • Finding meaning in ordinary moments
    • Sit with uncomfortable emotions
    • Hopefully finish a story and still think about it afterward
